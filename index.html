Before you go

What applies to all three days.

Passport, every time
Both parks use real-name tickets. Carry the physical passport; it’s checked at entry, and again on re-entry to the Forest Park on Day 3.
Out before dusk
Sunset is around 17:40 and the valleys go dark earlier. Published park hours disagree (closing at 17:00 or 18:00) and the last internal shuttle is around 18:00, so every plan here finishes by about 17:00.
Dress for two climates
The summits are noticeably colder and windier than town. Bring warm layers, gloves, non-slip shoes, and a phone light just in case.
Check the lifts the evening before
Cableways and the Bailong Elevator can pause for wind, ice or maintenance. Ask your hotel, or check the park’s official account, the night before each park day.
Book your Tianmen ride early
Morning taxis in Wulingyuan can be scarce. Book the Day 2 Didi (or ask the hotel) the night before, and arrange the ride back before you go up.
Bring lunch
Food on the plateau is limited and pricey. Pack lunch and water for Days 1 and 3.
Macaques
Wild monkeys live along Golden Whip Stream and at some viewpoints. Don’t feed them, and keep food out of sight.
About the student price
Some ticket sellers list full-time college students as eligible for a reduced package price, but it’s unclear whether a foreign student card qualifies. Your Trip.com ticket is the adult version.

Peaks, and how much stream?

Friday 20 November, through the Forest Park

Every version visits Tianzi Mountain and Yuanjiajie and uses the same two package rides. The difference is what fills the rest of the day: all of Golden Whip Stream, a short taste of it, or a ladder hike on the Yangjiajie plateau instead.

Our plan: Wulong Village. Both versions start the same way, up the Tianzi Cableway. Decide on top of Tianzi Mountain around 09:45: if the paths are wet, take the shuttle to Yuanjiajie instead and follow “All of it”, the full stream.

Tianmen Mountain, Line C

Saturday 21 November, up the cave stairs and around the summit

Since 13 October 2026 the long city cableway is closed, and Line C is the only route: express cableway from the Mountain Gate, escalators through the mountain, and back the same way. The hiking is all at the top. The escalators come out at a summit hub, and two cliff-edge trails, the West Line and the East Line, meet at Tianmen Temple. Walked together, they make a loop.

  • Walking about 4–4.5 h
  • Summit loop about 7.5 km
  • Transfer 50–70 min each way
  1. 07:30

    Didi to the Tianmen Mountain Gate

    Go straight to the Mountain Gate, not the old city cableway station. About 50–70 minutes, roughly ¥80–120.

  2. 08:45

    Ticket check at the Mountain Gate

    Passport needed. Tickets are sold for a time slot, and fast-track passes are suspended. It’s a Saturday, so expect crowds.

  3. 09:00

    Tianmen Cave Express Cableway up

    Included in Line C. Arrives at the square below the cave.

  4. 09:30to 10:30

    999 Steps to Tianmen Cave 天门洞

    A steep stair climb to the natural arch, the first real workout of the day. A separately charged escalator runs alongside (reportedly ¥32, unverified).

  5. 10:30

    Escalators to the summit hub

    A chain of escalators inside the mountain. Included.

  6. 10:45to 12:45

    West Line to Tianmen Temple

    About 4 km, 2 hours. Guigu Plank Road 鬼谷栈道, the West Line glass skywalk, Coiling Dragon Cliff glass walkway 盘龙崖, and a suspension bridge. Glass sections need shoe covers (about ¥5).

  7. 12:45

    Break at Tianmen Temple

    Bring snacks; food at the top is limited.

  8. 13:15to 14:45

    East Line back to the hub

    About 3.5 km, 1.5 hours, with viewpoints looking down on Tianmen Cave. The East Line glass walkway was reported closed for maintenance in May 2026; the trail itself stays open.

  9. 14:45

    Escalators and express cableway down

  10. ~15:30

    Didi back to Wulingyuan

    Back around 16:30–17:00. Arrange the pickup before you go up; drivers queue at the gate.

Separate ticket. Tianmen isn’t part of the Forest Park package. None of your package rides are used today.

Short on time or energy? The West Line has the headline cliff paths. It’s the one to keep if you can only walk half the loop.

Assumptions and checks

What changed from the first draft of this plan, and what is still unverified. Checked 25 October 2026.

  • Golden Whip Stream on Day 1, at a safer hourThe first draft ran it from 15:30 to 18:00, past sunset and the last shuttle. It now runs either 13:00–16:00 or first thing in the morning, depending on the start gate.
  • Day 1 is long by choiceAbout 5.5–6 hours of walking, above the 3–4 hours in the original brief, because the stream walk was a priority.
  • Day 1 alternativesWulong Village and One Step to Heaven are both reached from the Wulongzhai shuttle stop on the plateau, so either can replace the stream on Day 1. One Step to Heaven only fits if Yuanjiajie and Tianzi are shortened. Shuttle times between areas are estimates.
  • One Step to Heaven moved off the quick Day 1 stopFrom the nearest shuttle stop it is about 40 minutes plus an hour each way, with iron ladders at the end, so it doesn’t fit as a quick stop on Day 1.
  • Tianbo Mansion reported closedTrip.com lists it as closed with hours to be announced, and recent hiker reviews say its trail spurs are shut. It stays in the plan only as “if reopened”.
  • Tianmen summit loopThe West and East Lines both start at the escalator hub and meet at Tianmen Temple. Sources disagree on which line Coiling Dragon Cliff sits on; the most recent map puts it on the West Line. A May 2026 notice closed the East Line glass walkway for maintenance, and no reopening notice was found.
  • Ride count correctedEach lift ride counts one-way. The first draft counted Day 3 as one ride, although both of its options went up and down.
  • Package type unconfirmedTrip.com sells both four-ride and unlimited versions. One traveller with the same-named ticket reported unlimited rides. The rides section covers both cases.
  • Huangshizhai maintenance claim removedThe first draft said maintenance “ended 6 November”. No source confirmed this; it may be a mix-up with the Tianmen upper-cableway closure on 6 November 2025.
  • Park hours conflictSources give 07:00–18:00 (March to November) or 07:00/07:30–17:00. The plans here aim to be out by about 17:00.
  • Tianmen Line C confirmedLine C has been the only route since 13 October 2026, with timed tickets and no fast track. From Wulingyuan, go straight to the Mountain Gate.
  • Estimates to confirm on the dayShuttle times between areas, the Wulong Village and One Step to Heaven timings, the 999 Steps escalator (about ¥32), Didi fares, and the Chinese name for the Tianmen Mountain Gate.
